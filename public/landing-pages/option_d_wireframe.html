How it works

1. Film your expert (10 minutes)

Pull out your phone. Film your expert demonstrating the task. Same phone you use for Instagram. No production team, no fancy equipment, no scripts.

2. System creates the guide (10 minutes)

Upload the video. AI watches it, identifies the steps, extracts materials and tools, generates safety warnings, creates a complete step-by-step guide. You review and refine. Total time: 10 minutes.

3. Staff train on their phones

Assign the guide to new hires. They get a notification. They train during downtime—at home the night before, during a quiet afternoon, between shifts. Step-by-step. In their language. With instant AI help when stuck.

4. Training improves automatically

Staff flag issues during training: "Step 4 unclear," "Missing ingredient," "Better way to do this." You see all feedback in one dashboard. Fix problems once. Every future staff member gets the improved version. Training gets 30-50% better over the first 3 months.

That's it. No complexity. No ongoing maintenance. Just better training that keeps getting better.

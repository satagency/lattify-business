Case Study

London, 45 staff

The Problem

Head chef spent 8 hours training each new line cook. Trained 18 staff in 2024 = 144 hours of chef time. Inconsistent results, language barriers with Polish and Romanian staff.

The Solution

Filmed head chef making 12 signature dishes (2 hours total). System generated guides in English, Polish, Romanian. New hires train on phones during downtime.

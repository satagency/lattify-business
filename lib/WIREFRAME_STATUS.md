# Simple Black & White Wireframes - B2B Dashboard

## Design Philosophy
- **Simple wireframes** - clean, functional, black & white
- **Placeholder for dynamic experience** - actual platform will be much more dynamic
- **Focus on structure** - layout, information hierarchy, functionality
- **Keep it minimal** - no unnecessary decoration
